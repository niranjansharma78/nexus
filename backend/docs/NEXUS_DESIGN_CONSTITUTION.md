# Nexus Design Constitution

1. Decisions before data.
2. Calm before noise.
3. Systems of record remain authoritative.
4. External integrations are read-only.
5. Nexus earns trust before requesting control.
6. Connectors provide evidence; they do not define the product.
7. Business, Family, Personal and Digital Life are equal domains.
8. User layout is sacred.
9. Use progressive disclosure and controlled scrolling.
10. Confidence and uncertainty must be visible.
11. Nexus recommends; it does not impersonate certainty.
12. Enterprise automation is a separate future product.
