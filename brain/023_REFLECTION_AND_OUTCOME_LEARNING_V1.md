# ADR-023: Reflection and Outcome Learning v1

Status: Accepted
Brain version: 1.6.0 Alpha 1

Reflection converts outcomes into reusable experience.

## Core principles

- Expected and actual outcomes remain separate.
- Confidence changes are explicit.
- Failed assumptions are first-class data.
- Recommendation and chosen action are stored independently.
- Reflection can operate standalone or as part of Nexus LI.
- Lessons are persisted for future reasoning and learning.
