# ADR-029: Attention Engine v1

Status: Accepted  
Brain version: 2.2.0 Alpha 1

The Attention Engine determines what deserves cognitive resources now.

Inputs: importance, urgency, novelty, relevance, risk, confidence.

Outputs: attention score, priority, escalation decision, process-now decision.

Attention is temporary resource allocation. It is not identity and it is not long-term memory.
